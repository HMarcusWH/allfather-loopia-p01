## Owner separation

Existing P01/M05 evidence, AN-03 historical findings and AN-04 outcome reports are not automatically P04-released facts. AQ-02 treats NativeReleaseProposal and OutcomeAnnotation as metadata-only provenance. The successful fixture uses a separately named synthetic P01 released-output reference, not a fabricated diff or M10 release. Existing P04 Distribution/Release, M10 and M16 owners remain unchanged.

The only executable port in AQ-02 is the CLI-only fixed test implementation. It checks synthetic current reliance, rights, role and redaction, and labels stale/unknown rights, duplicates, human defer/suppress and role refusal explicitly. None of those fixture observations is real owner authentication.

The guard requires PHP CLI, a test-only constant, the exact test owner class and its source path. A real M10/P04/M16 owner adapter is not registered. The candidate carries no source text, credential, message, email address or customer entitlement.

## Digest and current reliance

InternalDigest.v1 composes one role, policy and period, with at most 64 input events, 16 members and explicit suppression entries for all excluded records. Members are priority-ordered with stable ties; over-cap and duplicate entries remain visible as suppression reasons.

The private synthetic rendering path rechecks source rights, M10 current reliance, role entitlement and redaction at display time for every included member. Later revocation or supersession stops rendering. A saved earlier PASS is never a new permission. There is no real P04 release, M16 activation, SMS, email, CRM or webhook dispatch.

## Proposed family340

Candidate SQL resides under docs/longitudinal/proposals, outside native migrations. Four tables store attention candidates, individual suppression events, digest candidates and digest membership. Eight update/delete triggers enforce append-only history. InternalAttentionStore has only a disposable CLI constructor; the runtime factory unconditionally refuses. It checks exact tenant/workspace/competitor/question scope, replay identity, duplicate attention keys, all digest members and refusal reasons. It writes no native release, decision or customer output rows.

The guarded operator entrypoint permits only status/help or a fixed synthetic development preview after an explicit test-only environment variable. It cannot select arbitrary tenant, source, SQL, model, recipient or execution channel.
